## Open items and safe assumptions

Default audience is global English; no market-specific traffic forecast. Hosting/framework is open, with static-first HTML and client processors required. No accounts, remote imports or automatic user-content storage. Provider-specific contact, logs, consent and ads are open until the owner supplies real details.

Manual Google market/device SERP and suggestion checks remain an acknowledged research limitation. They can refine priorities; they are not represented as completed. Paid metric validation requires authorized access to a named dataset. No login, external message, purchase or publication was performed.

## Change control

Before adding a tool, record primary owner, distinct task, existing-page overlap, local feasibility, privacy behavior, standards/fixture plan, content value and meaningful links. Revise the catalog, registry, sitemap/content count and documents together. An alternative implementation must preserve required user outcomes; do not silently weaken claims.
